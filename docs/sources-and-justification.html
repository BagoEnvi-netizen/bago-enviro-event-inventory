Sources and justification

The Event Inventory was not invented from scratch. Each part follows a published international or Caribbean method, so Tobago's records can be checked, compared and sent to ODPM and UNDRR without re-entry. Local fields were added only where Tobago's impact chains need them, and each local rule is named as such below.

Use this tab to answer "what is this based on?" and "why is it built this way?". The full design review, with every field and its basis, is in the Tobago Event Inventory: Review (Claude login required).

Standards the design follows

Standard Issued by What the inventory takes from it Where it shows
DesInventar disaster loss methodology UNDRR, UNDP The core rules: record every event with any impact, including small and medium ones; one record per event per place; always cite a source; most effect fields (deaths, injured, affected, evacuated, houses, roads, crops) Events, Sources
Sendai Framework indicators, targets A–D UNDRR The counts countries must report: deaths and missing, people affected, economic loss, damage to critical infrastructure and services; breakdown by sex, age and disability Events impact fields; Human effects
DELTA Resilience (successor to DesInventar Sendai) UNDRR, UNDP, WMO Cause and effect kept apart: one hazardous event linked to many effect records; a permanent unique record ID for exchange Hazardous events tab; hazardous_event_id; record_uuid
Global Disaster-Related Statistics Framework (G-DRSF) UN Statistical Commission with UNDRR, endorsed March 2026 One row per group of people or per asset, with dates for counts that change over time, such as displacement Human effects; Damage and losses
WMO Cataloguing of Hazardous Events WMO, through the Met Service A standard ID for each weather or climate event so impacts link to their cause Met Service IDs tab; wmo_che_id on Hazardous events
UNDRR–ISC Hazard Information Profiles, 2025 UNDRR, International Science Council Common hazard names and codes to map local event types to hip_name_or_code

Post-Disaster Needs Assessment (PDNA) Guidelines, Vol. A, including ECLAC DaLA EU, World Bank, UN Sector groups; damage, losses and additional costs kept apart; physical quantity recorded even without a cost; price year Damage and losses; est_cost_ttd and est_loss_ttd kept apart; price_year

Trinidad and Tobago Sargassum Management Plan (TTSMP) stoplight National Green, Yellow and Red stranding classes used for the sargassum entry rule sargassum_flag

Local additions for Tobago, used only where the impact chains need them: road closure hours and chainage (isolation, IC1), links to the coastal Site and Asset Register, the seven parishes, sargassum fields, and links to Risk Data Register entries (d031 landslides, d032 floods, d034 loss and damage, d056 road closures).

Design choices and why

Each rule below either follows a standard or is a local rule. Local rules are marked so the team knows which ones it can change.

Design choice Why Basis
Record every event with any impact, however small Small, frequent events add up to most of the damage and are rarely recorded elsewhere DesInventar
One row per event per place Lets each impact be mapped and counted on its own DesInventar
Cause (hazardous event) kept apart from effects (events) Links rainfall or swell to impacts for thresholds; counts storms as well as damage sites; matches national and UN reporting DELTA Resilience; WMO hazardous event catalogue
Every event cites at least one source Every number can be traced and checked DesInventar
Date and location precision recorded on every event Honest about uncertainty; only precise points are used to train susceptibility maps CHARIM
Verification ladder: Reported, Desk-verified, Partner-observed, Field-verified Shows how far each record can be trusted; only checked records enter the analysis. Desk-verified means one record from the mandated agency about a claim within its mandate, or two independent sources; a source that repeats another never counts as the second, because claims within a mandate cannot be checked independently Common inventory practice; Partner-observed step and the mandated-agency part of Desk-verified are a local rule (Draft 0.5; authority rule agreed 8 October 2026)
Field-verified set only by a CRVA team field check (form THA-FV-01, GPS point, two photos) Keeps a clear audit trail between the team's own checks and partner data Local rule: the standards require sources but do not define field verification
Who first saw it (data_origin) kept apart from how far it was checked (verification_status) A partner's field data is good evidence but is not the team's own check Local rule (Draft 0.5)
Damage, losses and additional costs kept apart, each with a price year Repair cost and lost income are different things; costs from different years can be adjusted for inflation PDNA / DaLA
Physical amounts recorded even without a cost Metres of road or number of houses are useful long before a cost is known PDNA
Totals on the event; detail rows may not exceed them Stops double counting in reports Local QA rule (Review point 3)
Fields grouped as Essential, Recommended and Loss & damage A 3–4 person team fills what Tier 1 needs first Local rule (Draft 0.4)
No names of affected people; consent recorded for community information Privacy and trust in communities Local rule; full privacy rules still open (I11)
climate_related set from the trigger; attribution only from a published study Separates climate from non-climate losses without claiming attribution the unit cannot prove Loss and damage reporting practice
Sargassum recorded only when Yellow or Red, or Green with an impact Sargassum strands most weeks in season; the coastal unit keeps the full record, so no evidence is lost Local rule (Draft 0.5); a stated departure from the DesInventar "every event" rule

Unit decisions and open questions

The team has settled three design questions: H1, H3, and the Desk-verified authority rule (8 October 2026), under which a mandated agency's record within its mandate is enough on its own and a repeat never counts as a second source. Several others are still open, and the inventory uses interim rules until they are decided.

Item Status What it means for the inventory
H1, option B: IDs never renumbered Decided (assessment of the alternate review (Claude login required)) The year in an ID is the event year when the ID was issued. A later date correction leaves the ID alone.
H3, option A: no countable impact goes in damage_desc Decided (same document) "No impact beyond the hazard: …" in damage_desc counts as the impact field
I4: what counts as a significant event Open Interim: any death, any building destroyed, a road closed more than a day, or any THA response spending
I5: interim hazardous event ID scheme Open HE-YYYY-NNN used until the Met Service issues WMO IDs; the Met Service IDs tab is ready to cross-reference them without renumbering
I11: privacy rules Open No names of affected people; consent recorded
I23: local hazard code for sargassum Open Needed before export to DELTA; to settle with ODPM
I24: consistency of the coastal unit's stoplight flags Open Sargassum entry rule relies on the flags
I25: minimum precise events per hazard before a susceptibility map counts as trained Open QA shows the training-ready count
When a field check is required Proposal for discussion (proposal (Claude login required)) No change until agreed

All open items are tracked in the CRVA Open Questions Log.

What the inventory feeds

Every field earns its place by feeding at least one CRVA output. The live page shows what each field feeds under its help line.

CRVA output What it uses from the inventory Why it matters
Hazard map validation Coordinates, location precision, size and depth A missing or misplaced event makes a map look better or worse than it is
Landslide and flood susceptibility maps Precise points only (GPS or road chainage), event type Imprecise points are left out
Screening matrix likelihood and consequence Event type, trigger, dates; deaths, people affected, buildings How often and how badly each hazard hits sets its score
Impact chains Road closure hours (IC1), livelihoods and boats (IC3), salinity reports (IC8), parent_event_id Shows whether the chains actually happen in Tobago
Thresholds and DAPP signposts hazardous_event_id with rainfall, wind or water level; slow-onset values Shows when an adaptation pathway must change
Exposure and vulnerability Facilities affected, human effects by group, ecosystem and non-economic losses Who and what is hit hardest
Loss and damage record (register d034) Damage and losses with price year, response funding National and UN reporting; evidence for Loss and Damage Fund requests
