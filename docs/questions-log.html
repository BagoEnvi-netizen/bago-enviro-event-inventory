Tobago CRVA Open Questions Log

Oct 2, 2026 · Climate Unit

How to use this log

This log holds the questions raised while refining the Tier 1 method that the unit cannot answer alone, or must settle as a team before work goes ahead. It sits beside Tobago Tier 1 CRVA plan and Tobago Tier 1 Data Collection Campaign: 9-Month Plan. Decisions already listed in the plan's “Decisions and open questions” section are not repeated here.

Columns. Q: a fixed number that never changes. Question: what needs an answer. Why it matters: what is blocked or affected. Register: the related Risk Data Register IDs (d = dataset, m = method). Owner: the unit member who follows it up. Status: Open, Asked (sent, awaiting reply), Answered (reply received, not yet acted on), Closed. Next step: the next concrete action and when.

Working rules. Add new questions at the bottom of the right table with the next free number (E for external, I for internal). Ask several questions to the same organization in one letter or meeting. When a question is closed, move it to Resolved with the answer, the date and where it was recorded. Review the open items at the weekly team meeting and before each campaign gate.

Inventory extract. This log is internal to the Climate Unit. The questions that affect people who record or read events (I4, I5, I11, I23, I24, I25, I26 and I27) are copied, with a rule to follow until each is settled, to Event Inventory: Open Questions, which the inventory's users can read. When one of those questions changes or closes, or a new inventory question is added, update that page on the same day.

Month 1 letters. Most external questions marked “Month 1” should go in the formal data request letters (campaign workstream J), so partners answer them in one round.

I20 Sargassum stranding classes: which scale does the unit use? The national TTSMP guidance report (UNDP, 2026, p. 62–67) sets a Stoplight Classification with photos: Green under 5 cm, Yellow 15–22 in (about 38–56 cm), Red 1 m or more, plus a nearshore version. Its depth bands leave gaps (5–38 cm and 56–100 cm) and mix units. The THA Sargassum Emergency Response Plan uses different height bands (under 0.1 m, 0.1–0.6 m, over 0.6 m) in its Response Index for Tier 1–3. Agree with the Coastal Unit how the unit's classes 0–3 map to both, and propose closing the gaps Field and drone records must translate into the response tiers the THA already uses Segmentation and drone criteria; Sargassum plan Coastal Unit Open Before coastal appraisal (month 3)
