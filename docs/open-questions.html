Event Inventory: Open Questions

Oct 13, 2026 · Climate Team

What this page is

These are the open questions that affect people who record or read events in the Tobago Event Inventory. They are taken from the Climate Unit's full open questions log for the Tobago CRVA, which stays internal. Each question keeps the same number it has in the full log.

Until a question is settled, follow the rule in the third column. When it is settled, the decision goes into the instruction manual and the question moves to Resolved below.

Open questions

Q Question What to do until it is settled Decide by
I4 What counts as a significant event, so that the loss and damage detail tabs are filled? Fill the detail tabs only for events marked significant on the Events tab. The inventory applies an interim rule. Before desk mining starts
I5 Which ID scheme do hazardous events use until the Met Service issues official IDs? Use the inventory's own IDs (HE-YYYY-NNN). Official Met Service IDs are matched later on the Met Service IDs tab. Month 1
I11 Privacy rules for household and disaggregated data: how it is stored, who can see it and what is published Record no names, phone numbers or identifiable faces anywhere in the inventory. Before parish sessions
I23 Which local hazard code does sargassum get, mapped to the nearest international profile (UNDRR-ISC BI0603, not an exact match)? Record sargassum with the event type Sargassum influx. The code will be added to all records once agreed. Before desk mining starts
I24 Are the Coastal Zone Management Unit's Green, Yellow and Red sargassum flags applied the same way across beaches, officers and weeks? Enter a sargassum event only when the beach is flagged Yellow or Red, or Green with an impact. Before the next peak season
I25 How many verified, precisely located events of each hazard are needed before a landslide or flood susceptibility map counts as trained? Record locations as precisely as you can and say how they were obtained. The Overview tab shows how many events are precise enough. Before susceptibility model training
I26 Once WhatsApp photos are saved to the restricted folder, must staff delete them from their phones and chats, and how soon? Save the evidence to the restricted folder promptly, and do not forward it to anyone. Before WhatsApp reports are collected routinely
I27 How long does the restricted WhatsApp folder keep material, and who reviews and clears it? Do not delete any file a source links to. Before WhatsApp reports are collected routinely
